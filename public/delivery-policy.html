Delivery Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

BHADEX PLUGS aims to make digital product delivery simple, reliable and convenient. This policy explains how we handle orders after payment.

1. Delivery Time

Our delivery target is within 24 hours after successful payment confirmation.

This is a target rather than a guaranteed delivery time. Availability issues, provider delays and technical problems may sometimes cause delays.

2. Digital Delivery

Products purchased through BHADEX PLUGS are digital. Depending on the product, delivery may involve account access, activation instructions, a digital code or other electronic information.

We will communicate the applicable delivery method for your order.

3. Payment Confirmation

Orders are processed after successful payment confirmation. A pending, failed or unverified payment may delay order processing.

You can use your order reference and purchase email to check your order status on our tracking page.

Track Your Order

4. Customer Information

Customers should provide accurate contact information when placing an order.

Incorrect email addresses or other missing details may delay delivery. Contact support if you notice an error in your order information.

5. Delayed Orders

If your order has not arrived within the 24-hour delivery target, contact our support team.

Please provide your order reference and the email address used for the purchase so we can investigate.

6. Product Availability

Delivery depends on the availability of the product or service ordered. If we cannot fulfil an order, we will contact the customer to discuss an appropriate resolution.

Eligible refund requests are handled according to our Refund Policy.
