Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about BHADEX PLUGS.

1. What is BHADEX PLUGS?

BHADEX PLUGS is an online marketplace where customers can explore and request available digital products and services.

2. How do I place an order?

Visit our marketplace, select an available product and follow the checkout instructions to place your order.

3. Which payment methods are available?

Payments on our website are processed through Paystack. The payment methods displayed at checkout depend on the options supported for your transaction.

4. How long does delivery take?

Our delivery target is within 24 hours after successful payment confirmation. Delays may occur because of availability or technical issues.

5. How can I track my order?

Visit our Track Order page and enter your order reference and purchase email address.

Track Your Order

6. What if my payment succeeds but my order is not delivered?

Contact our support team with your order reference and purchase email so we can investigate.

7. Can I request a refund?

Yes. BHADEX PLUGS offers refunds in eligible situations. Please read our refund policy for details.
