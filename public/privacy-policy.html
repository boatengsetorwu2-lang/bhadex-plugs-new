PRIVACY POLICY.

This Privacy Policy explains how BHADEX PLUGS collects, uses, stores and protects information when you visit our website or use our services.

Effective date: 23 November 2026

1. About BHADEX PLUGS

BHADEX PLUGS is an independent digital services marketplace. This policy applies to information collected through our website and related customer support services.

For privacy questions, contact us at bhadexplugs@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may collect:

  • Account information: Your name, email address and password information when you register.
  • Order information: Products selected, order references, order amounts and order status.
  • Payment information: Payment references and transaction status returned by our payment provider.
  • Verification information: Email verification codes and related verification records.
  • Support information: Information you choose to provide when contacting our support team.
  • Technical information: Certain technical information may be processed by our hosting and service providers when you use the website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use information where necessary to:

  • Create and manage customer accounts.
  • Verify customer email addresses.
  • Process and manage orders.
  • Confirm and verify payment transactions.
  • Send order confirmations and account-related emails.
  • Help customers track their orders.
  • Respond to customer questions and support requests.
  • Protect the website against misuse and unauthorised activity.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Payments and Third-Party Services

Our website uses Paystack for payment processing. Payments are handled through the payment provider's checkout system.

We may receive payment references, transaction status and other information needed to confirm an order. Payment providers may process payment information under their own privacy policies and terms.

We also use service providers for website hosting, database storage and transactional email delivery. These providers may process information as needed to provide their services.

We do not control the independent privacy practices of third-party providers. Customers should review their privacy policies.

5. Passwords and Account Security

Customer passwords are stored using password-hashing protection rather than being stored as readable passwords.

We use account verification and security measures intended to help protect customer accounts. Customers should keep their login details private and contact us if they suspect unauthorised access.

6. Email Communications

We may send emails relating to account verification, password recovery, order confirmation and important service updates.

These messages are necessary for operating your account or completing requested services.

7. Cookies and Sessions

Our website may use session-related technologies to keep customers signed in and support secure website functions.

Your browser may allow you to control cookies through its settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect some website features.

8. Sharing of Information

We do not sell customer personal information. We may share necessary information with service providers who help operate our website, process payments, deliver emails or provide technical support.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where necessary to protect our customers, website or legal rights.

9. Data Storage and Retention

Customer information is stored using the systems and service providers used to operate BHADEX PLUGS.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, manage orders, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and applicable requirements.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, request corrections, ask about how it is used or request deletion where legally permitted.

To make a privacy request, contact bhadexplugs@gmail.com.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without the required consent or legal basis. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, systems or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with its effective date.
