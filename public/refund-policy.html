Refund Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

At BHADEX PLUGS, we want customers to receive the digital products and services they have ordered. This policy explains how refund requests are reviewed and handled.

1. When You May Request a Refund

You may contact us to request a refund in situations such as:

  • You paid successfully but we cannot supply the ordered service.
  • You were charged more than once for the same order.
  • We supplied a product or service different from what you ordered.
  • There is another reasonable issue with the order that needs investigation.

Each request is reviewed using the order information and the circumstances of the problem.

2. How to Request a Refund

Contact our support team and provide:

  • Your order reference.
  • The email address used for the purchase.
  • A description of the problem.
  • Any relevant payment confirmation or supporting information.

Never send us your card PIN, password or one-time payment code.

3. Review of Refund Requests

We will review the payment record, order details and delivery status before making a decision.

If we need more information, our support team may contact you. We will communicate the outcome of the review.

4. Duplicate Payments

If you believe you were charged more than once for one order, contact us with the relevant payment references. We will investigate the transactions.

5. Unavailable or Undelivered Orders

Our delivery target is within 24 hours after successful payment confirmation.

If we cannot supply an order or it remains undelivered, contact support. We will review the situation and discuss the appropriate resolution, which may include a refund.

6. Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, we will explain the applicable processing method.

The time it takes for money to appear in your account may depend on the payment provider and financial institution.
